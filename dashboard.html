Long engine

  1. Buy a slice whenever the price drops STEP below the lowest price currently held.
  2. Sell each slice the moment it is up STEP from what it was bought for.
  3. Never hold more than five slices at once.
  4. If a slice has been held too long and is down too much, sell it anyway.
  5. Buy smaller than usual on any day that moves far more than normal.

Hold

  1. Buy once at the start. Hold forever.

Short engine

  1. Short a slice whenever the price rises STEP above the highest price currently shorted.
  2. Cover each slice the moment it is down STEP from where it was shorted.
  3. Never hold more than five short slices at once.
  4. If a slice has been open too long and is losing, cover it anyway.
  5. Short smaller than usual on any day that moves far more than normal.
  6. Cover immediately if a slice is down past the hard stop, regardless.

Settings

Simulated only. No real money and no orders are placed. Costs are modelled at two basis points of slippage each way, the SEC fee at $20.60 per million dollars of proceeds, and the FINRA fee at $0.000195 per share sold. Settings were chosen by resampling Cerebras' own daily returns into four hundred synthetic paths, not by fitting to the single real history shown here.
